# Baby Growth Video Project

This folder is a ready-to-fill project kit for a 5-6 minute first-birthday growth video.

## Target

- Length: about 5 minutes 30 seconds
- Format: MP4, 1080p, 16:9 horizontal
- Tone: warm and emotional
- Caption style: short Korean captions
- Recommended music: 1 calm song, or 2 songs with a natural mood change

## Folder Use

Place selected photos and short clips into these folders:

- `assets/00_opening`: ultrasound, pregnancy, baby name, first portrait
- `assets/01_birth_newborn`: birth, hospital, newborn, small hands and feet
- `assets/02_1-3_months`: early smiles, being held, tummy time
- `assets/03_4-6_months`: rolling over, laughing, toys, daily life
- `assets/04_7-9_months`: sitting, crawling, meals, outings
- `assets/05_10-12_months`: standing, walking practice, recent portraits
- `assets/06_family_ending`: family photos and final birthday image
- `exports`: final rendered files

## Editing Rules

- Use 70-90 photos total.
- Use 10-18 video clips total.
- Keep each video clip around 3-6 seconds.
- Keep photos on screen around 2.5-4 seconds.
- Use soft fade transitions and subtle zoom only.
- Avoid repeating very similar photos.
- For vertical media, use blurred side background or a consistent frame treatment.
- Keep the final video under 6 minutes.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Put all raw media into the matching monthly folders.
2. Pick only the strongest photos and clips for each section.
3. Follow `timeline.csv` for timing.
4. Use `captions.md` for short captions.
5. Render a first draft and watch it on a TV or full-screen laptop.
6. Check readable captions, audio volume, image quality, and whether the video feels too long.
