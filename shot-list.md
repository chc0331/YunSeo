# Shot List

Use this as a selection checklist before editing.

## Opening: 5-7 items

- [ ] Baby name or title image
- [ ] Ultrasound or pregnancy photo
- [ ] First baby portrait
- [ ] Parent and baby detail shot
- [ ] Calm close-up image

## Birth and Newborn: 12-15 items

- [ ] Birth day photo
- [ ] Hospital or postpartum care center photo
- [ ] Baby sleeping
- [ ] Small hands
- [ ] Small feet
- [ ] First family photo
- [ ] Parent holding baby
- [ ] 1-2 short newborn clips

## 1-3 Months: 14-17 items

- [ ] First smiles
- [ ] Being held by family
- [ ] Tummy time
- [ ] Sleeping face
- [ ] Bath or daily care
- [ ] 2-3 short clips

## 4-6 Months: 14-17 items

- [ ] Rolling over
- [ ] Laughing face
- [ ] Favorite toy
- [ ] Family daily life
- [ ] Seasonal photo
- [ ] 2-4 short clips

## 7-9 Months: 16-20 items

- [ ] Sitting alone
- [ ] Crawling
- [ ] Eating baby food
- [ ] Outing
- [ ] Playful expression
- [ ] 3-5 short clips

## 10-12 Months: 14-17 items

- [ ] Standing with support
- [ ] Walking practice
- [ ] Recent portrait
- [ ] Dol photo
- [ ] Favorite expression
- [ ] 2-4 short clips

## Ending: 5-7 items

- [ ] Best family photo
- [ ] Birthday portrait
- [ ] Smiling close-up
- [ ] Final message background photo
